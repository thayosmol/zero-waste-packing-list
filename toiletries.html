Plastic-Free & Solid Toiletries Guide

Skip the airport liquid limits and plastic bottles with these eco-friendly swaps.

Solid Shampoo & Conditioner Bars

These concentrated bars last for months, take up zero liquid quota at airport security, and come wrapped in paper instead of plastic bottles. Tip: Let them dry completely before putting them in a tin container.

Toothpaste Tablets

Instead of bulky plastic tubes, toothpaste tablets come in glass jars or compostable pouches. Simply chew a tablet, brush with a wet toothbrush, and rinse!

Safety Razor

Throw away disposable plastic razors forever. A sturdy metal safety razor gives a closer shave, uses cheap recyclable metal blades, and looks great in your travel kit. *(Note: Pack blades in your checked luggage!)*
