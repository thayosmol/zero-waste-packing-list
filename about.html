About This Project

Welcome! EcoPack was created out of a simple love for travel and a growing frustration with how much plastic waste standard tourism generates. From tiny shampoo bottles left in hotel rooms to single-use plastic water bottles bought on day trips, travel can easily leave a heavy footprint.

Our Mission

Our goal is to prove that traveling sustainably doesn't mean sacrificing convenience or comfort. By curating the best zero-waste gear, straightforward packing guides, and plastic-free product reviews, we want to help you explore the globe while preserving it for generations to come.
